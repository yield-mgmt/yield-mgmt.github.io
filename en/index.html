How an engagement runs

Three steps from diagnosis to measurable results

Every engagement follows the same path, and each step ends with a clear deliverable.

Step 1

Research

What we do
Review the organizational structure, people and current processes through interviews and an AI-assisted review of your systems.
What you get
A full organizational diagnostic focused on your specific challenges.
Step 2

Root cause analysis

What we do
Identify the main bottlenecks and pain points: manual, error-prone and time-consuming work, and gaps in control, communication and ownership.
What you get
A root cause report and a suggested action plan.
Step 3

Execution

What we do
Support the implementation of the agreed plan: milestones, roles and responsibilities, KPIs, process improvement and automation, a sustainable plan and ROI.
What you get
Lower costs, KPIs achieved, better effectiveness and customer satisfaction.

Questions and answers

Common questions about YIELD

Short answers on what YIELD does, who it is for, how an engagement works and how to start.

What does YIELD MGMT Consulting do?

YIELD MGMT Consulting provides leadership development and organizational consulting for growing tech companies: manager development workshops, 1:1 programs, process improvement, and AI tools such as Anthropic Claude and Microsoft Copilot for organizational knowledge management and automation.

Who is YIELD for?

CEOs, VPs, HR leaders and other senior decision-makers in small and mid-size growth companies, mainly in tech. Programs are also available for managers at every level, from first-line to executive.

How does an engagement work?

In three steps: research (interviews and a review of structure, people and processes), root cause analysis (bottlenecks and a suggested action plan), and execution (milestones, KPIs and ROI).
